# POL-005 Referrals among commonly owned providers

> Synthetic policy written for this prototype. Not a real payer policy.

Referrals among providers with common ownership must be clinically justified in the record and disclosed. Patterns of circular referral on a shared member group trigger a network-level review.

Public basis to verify: Physician self-referral (Stark) law and federal Anti-Kickback Statute, as general background
