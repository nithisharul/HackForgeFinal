# POL-IN-003 Admission criteria for conditions treatable as outpatients

> Synthetic policy written for this prototype. Not a real scheme or insurer policy.

Admission is payable only when the condition required inpatient care, documented with admission vitals, investigations and treatment notes. Short admissions for outpatient-treatable conditions trigger a medical audit.

Public basis to verify: NHA anti-fraud triggers (OPD-to-IPD conversion is an official trigger; no threshold is published)
