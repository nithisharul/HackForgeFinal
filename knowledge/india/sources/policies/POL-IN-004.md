# POL-IN-004 Beneficiary identity verification

> Synthetic policy written for this prototype. Not a real scheme or insurer policy.

Every admission requires Aadhaar-based beneficiary authentication and a patient photograph at admission and discharge. Cards created shortly before a claim and shared contact details trigger beneficiary verification by phone or home visit.

Public basis to verify: CAG Report 11 of 2023 (lakhs of beneficiaries on one placeholder mobile number); NHA Anti-Fraud Framework Practitioners' Guidebook (2020)
