# POL-IN-007 Package, ward and rate selection

> Synthetic policy written for this prototype. Not a real scheme or insurer policy.

The package, ward and rate claimed must match the documented diagnosis, the patient and the hospital's own tier and accreditation. ICU days must be supported by the ICU register and vitals charts. A first upcoding offence can be penalised up to 10 times the excess claimed.

Public basis to verify: NHA Anti-Fraud Framework Practitioners' Guidebook (2020) on upcoding penalties; HBP 2.0 and HBP 2.2 package, ward and incentive rates (5 of the 45 package rates used here are estimates, not verified HBP rates)
