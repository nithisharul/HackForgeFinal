# POL-IN-008 One beneficiary, one admission at a time

> Synthetic policy written for this prototype. Not a real scheme or insurer policy.

A beneficiary can be an inpatient at only one hospital at a time, except for a documented transfer with discharge from the first hospital. Overlapping stays are checked against both hospitals' registers.

Public basis to verify: CAG Report 11 of 2023 (claims with overlapping admissions)
