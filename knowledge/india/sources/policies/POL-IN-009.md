# POL-IN-009 Claims for deceased beneficiaries

> Synthetic policy written for this prototype. Not a real scheme or insurer policy.

No claim is payable for an admission after the beneficiary's recorded death. Every such claim is audited, as are all in-hospital deaths.

Public basis to verify: CAG Report 11 of 2023 (claims for patients already recorded as dead); NHA audit norm of auditing every death case
