# Claims after recorded death

## Definition
A claim is made for a beneficiary after the date of death recorded against their card.

## Detection signals
- Rule IN3: admission after the beneficiary's recorded date of death

## Policy basis
- POL-IN-009 Claims for deceased beneficiaries (`knowledge/india/sources/policies/POL-IN-009.md`)
- Public basis: CAG Report 11 of 2023 (claims for patients already recorded as dead); NHA audit norm of auditing every death case

## Known innocent explanations
- A wrong date of death in the beneficiary database, corrected with the death certificate
