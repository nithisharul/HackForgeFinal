# Anti-Kickback Statute

> Reference page written by code from the pipeline. Rebuilt on every change; do not edit by hand.

> General background written for this prototype. It is a short summary, not legal advice; verify against the source.

## What it is
United States federal criminal law (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b)) that prohibits offering or receiving anything of value to induce or reward referrals of services paid by federal health care programmes.

## How this system uses it
Background for the collusive network pattern. Referral loops among related providers are a reason to look, not proof of a kickback.

## Source to verify
- 42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b); safe harbours at 42 CFR 1001.952.
