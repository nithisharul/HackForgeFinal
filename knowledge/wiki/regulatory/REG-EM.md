# Evaluation and management (office visit) coding guidance

> Reference page written by code from the pipeline. Rebuilt on every change; do not edit by hand.

> General background written for this prototype. It is a short summary, not legal advice; verify against the source.

## What it is
Office visits are billed with CPT codes 99211-99215. The level must be supported by documented medical decision making or total time on the date of the visit.

## How this system uses it
Basis for [[R5]] and the upcoding pattern. A high level mix triggers a records review, not a denial.
